What Is Ceramic Coating?

Not a Wax. Not a Sealant.
Something Better.

Ceramic coating is a liquid nano-ceramic polymer that chemically bonds to your vehicle's paint. Unlike wax or paint sealants that sit on top of the surface and wash away over time, ceramic coating becomes part of the paint — creating a hard, semi-permanent protective layer that lasts for years.

The result? Water, dirt, bird droppings, UV rays, and minor scratches simply can't get through. Your car stays cleaner longer, shines brighter, and holds its value better.

💧
Hydrophobic — Water Beads Off
Water sheets off on contact. No more water spots, mineral deposits, or that dull haze after rain.
☀️
UV Blocking — Prevents Fading
Michigan summers are brutal on paint. Ceramic blocks UV rays and prevents oxidation and color fade.
🛡️
Scratch Resistant Surface
The 9H hardness rating resists light swirl marks and surface scratches from normal wash cycles.
✨
Deep, Lasting Gloss
The nano-ceramic layer amplifies your paint's reflectivity — creating a wet, mirror-like shine that doesn't fade.
🧹
Self-Cleaning Effect
Dirt and contaminants can't bond to the surface. A quick rinse is often all you need between washes.

Yes. We apply ceramic coating to cars, trucks, SUVs, and specialty vehicles. Paint condition matters — vehicles with heavy oxidation or deep scratches will need paint correction first. We'll assess your vehicle and recommend the right package when you call.

Wax sits on top of your paint and washes away within weeks. Ceramic coating chemically bonds to the paint surface, creating a semi-permanent protective layer that's far harder, longer-lasting, and more resistant to heat, UV, and chemicals. It's not comparable — it's a category above.
